About

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Computing Science at Simon Fraser University, where I’m fortunate to work under the supervision of Professor Mo Chen at the MARS Lab. Before joining SFU, I completed my Master’s degree at Sharif University of Technology, advised by Professor Mahdieh Soleymani Baghshah, and earned my Bachelor’s degree at Shiraz University.

I’m interested in how agents can learn efficiently from experience and transfer what they know to new situations. More specifically, my research focuses on decision-making agents that keep learning and adapting under non-stationarity. I work on reinforcement learning, credit assignment, continual learning, and meta-RL, often drawing on ideas from neuroscience.
